# NE0SYNC Writeups

Technical notes on reverse engineering, application internals and offensive
research. Each write-up focuses on the path from first observation to a
supported finding.

## Contents

| Write-up | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Electron ASAR Analysis](./electron-asar-analysis.md) | Electron packaging, entitlement flow and client-side trust boundaries |

## Reading the write-ups

The notes are written as investigations rather than step-by-step bypass guides.
They record:

- the initial hypothesis;
- the architecture and relevant components;
- static and runtime observations;
- evidence that confirmed or rejected the hypothesis;
- the technical finding and its impact;
- the engineering change that would remove the weakness.

All examples are discussed within an authorized research context. No binaries,
credentials, live targets or reusable bypass instructions are included.
